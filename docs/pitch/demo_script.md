# KAVACH — 3 Minute Demo Script

## 0:00 — Problem

Today, if someone needs to prove one fact about themselves,
they often have to hand over an entire document.

A bank statement doesn't just contain income.
It contains an account number, transactions and other private information.

KAVACH changes that.

## 0:25 — Private Brain

This is KAVACH running locally.

These are the user's documents, notes and conversations.

KAVACH ingests them into a private knowledge base.

It understands not just individual documents, but the relationships
between people, projects, decisions and facts.

## 0:55 — Ask My Vault

I can ask:

"What was my decision about renewing the flat?"

KAVACH retrieves the relevant information and cites its sources.

The user can see exactly where the answer came from.

## 1:20 — Third Party Request

Now I'm switching to the requester.

The requester asks:

"Does the tenant earn at least ₹50,000 per month?"

KAVACH does not send the bank statement.

It proposes the minimum disclosure.

The owner approves it.

The requester receives the verification result and can verify its authenticity.

## 1:55 — Privacy Boundary

Now the requester asks for information that isn't necessary.

KAVACH refuses instead of exposing the underlying document.

The user's data stays behind the privacy boundary.

The interaction is also recorded in the audit trail.

## 2:15 — Memory

Now we introduce new information.

The original decision was:

"Renew only if rent stays below ₹15,000."

The landlord now says rent will become ₹16,000 from January.

KAVACH ingests the new information.

When we ask about the current situation, it can reason over the newer information
and show that the previous condition is no longer satisfied.

## 2:40 — Close

That's KAVACH.

Not another chatbot.

It's a sovereign second brain that can understand your knowledge,
remember it, reason over it, and expose only what you choose.
